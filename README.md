# adv_interface_wk10

Advanced Interface, week 10: Navigation.

**[Open the overview of all sets](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk10/)**

| Set | Tone | Concept | Prototype |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mysterious | **Axis Nav**: 93 objects from The Met, placed by where they were made and when. The top bar is *where* (four hemispheres on a longitude scale), the left rail is *when* (centuries), the bottom row is *how* you look (a material quadrant, then Front, Side, Top). Every item on every bar is the same small circle; the chosen one wears a compass ring, and the rest grow smaller and fainter the farther they are from the focus. Collapsed, the bars are only these marks; tap the circle at a bar's start and its axis is drawn out across the page with names and counts (a small frame ×n). Choosing is drawn like a compass; dotted lines run from the bars to the real pieces in the world, each ringed; pointing at a piece lights its century and marks its longitude. Picking a material quadrant zooms into that quarter of the cross| [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk10/axis-nav/) |
| 2 | Playful | **Dive Log · Foam**: every item is a bubble; bubbles in one group share their walls like soap foam, and the expanded sidebar is the same foam stretched sideways into petals. Collapse and the petals pull back into round bubbles; hover and a bubble inflates and shoves its neighbours, which settle back; selected fills mint and glows. The page is built from the same foam: a big bubble holds the main number (3 new dives synced from the dive computer), the dive sites sit on its surface as smaller bubbles sharing its wall, tap one and the big number becomes its depth. The action (Add notes) is a petal under the big bubble or a slice cut off its bottom. Log a dive can be a row, a seed that stretches into a petal, a bud with a shortcut satellite, or four small bubbles that merge into one plus. Black, taupe and mint on haze | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk10/dive-log/) |
| 3 | Serene | **Breath · Foam**: the same foam system, slowed down. Every bubble breathes at its exercise's pace (6, 10, 30 breaths a minute), the big bubble breathes with the suggested exercise and the duration bubbles stay attached to it as it swells and shrinks. Pick 1, 3 or 5 minutes and press Start: the other bubbles deflate, the big bubble counts down and reads Breathe in · Hold · Breathe out in time with its own size; End brings the page back. Ten ways to show the breath inside the big bubble, from light (glow, mesh, tide, ripple, sunrise) to flat shapes (none, inner circle, level, micro-foam, count). Lime on warm dark | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk10/breath/) |

Each set lives in its own folder with a self-contained index.html. Spec is inside each prototype (Spec button).
