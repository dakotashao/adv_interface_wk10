# adv_interface_wk10

Advanced Interface, week 10: Navigation.

| Set | Tone | Concept | Prototype |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mysterious | **Axis Nav**: 93 objects from The Met, placed by where they were made and when. The top bar is *where* (four hemispheres on a longitude scale), the left rail is *when* (centuries on a time scale), the view bar is *how* you look (Front, Side, Top). Both bars work like drawing on paper: collapsed, they are only marks, with the chosen item sharp and the rest going softer the farther they are (focus); tap the small circle at a bar's end and its axis is drawn out across the whole page, every century with its count and a colour strip made from its pieces. Choosing is drawn with a compass: the old ring winds back, the new one is drawn from the side it came from. Pointing at a century shows its representative piece in a ring, and a dotted line runs from the bar to where those pieces really are in the world; empty centuries are open marks and are stepped over; scroll or drag along the rail to move through time. Hovering a piece draws a ring around it; holding one draws a ring first, the picture settles in, and turning it over winds the ring back and writes the label line by line | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk10/axis-nav/) |

Each set lives in its own folder with a self-contained index.html.
