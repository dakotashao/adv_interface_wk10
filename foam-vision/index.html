foam

A design system made of one shape: a bubble you can hold.

Every component is a bubble, a petal or foam. They share walls, push each other and make room, so the interface behaves like one soft material instead of separate boxes.

One shape

A bubble. Stretched, it becomes a petal: rows, bars, buttons. Packed, it becomes foam: groups that share walls.

Things touch

Hover, press and select are physical. A bubble swells and its neighbours move or shrink to make room.

Tone through motion

The same parts read playful or serene by changing speed, springiness and colour, never the shape.
